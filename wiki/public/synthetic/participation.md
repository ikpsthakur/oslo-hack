# Synthetic participation scenarios

These are fictional policy examples, not adopted Oslo Hack policies or guarantees. Status of every record: sample-only. The actual host must confirm arrangements before publication as real facts. Always label answers as a fictional demo. See [store rules](index.md).

## POLICY-001 — Language

Example policy: use English for shared introductions and let small groups use English or Norwegian by agreement. This demonstrates a possible approach only; the actual session language has not been confirmed.

## POLICY-002 — Accessibility enquiries

Example process: ask the organiser about step-free entry, lifts, accessible toilets, noise, and seating before attending. No organiser contact or venue facilities have been verified, and the assistant must not claim that the sample rooms are accessible.

## POLICY-003 — Respectful collaboration

Example policy: take turns, explain unfamiliar terms, welcome beginners, and ask before editing someone else's work. If a concern arises, a hypothetical host would help. This is not evidence that staff or a reporting channel is available.

## POLICY-004 — Photos and project sharing

Example policy: obtain consent before photographing or crediting someone; let teams choose whether to publish their work. No real consent, photo permission, intellectual-property agreement, or release form is represented by this dataset.

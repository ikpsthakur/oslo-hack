# Experiment and insight cards

These fictional prompts are creative exercises, not research findings or measured Oslo Hack outcomes. Every record is sample-only. Say “In this fictional demo…” and describe them as possibilities to test, not established facts. See [store rules](index.md).

## INSIGHT-001 — The paper-first twist

Experiment: represent a planned app with five paper cards and ask someone to arrange the next step. Possibility: discover confusing labels before spending time on code. Evidence to collect: one observed hesitation, not a claim that paper always outperforms software. Try [PROJECT-004](projects.md).

## INSIGHT-002 — One dataset, two views

Experiment: reuse five fictional activity records as cards and as a simple list. Possibility: different visitors may prefer different ways to scan the same information. Compare one task without assuming a winner. Try [PROJECT-002](projects.md); mock data does not become real through reuse.

## INSIGHT-003 — Swap the roles

Experiment: a designer navigates the code changes while a coder tests the words on screen, then reverse. Possibility: reveal assumptions each person normally misses. Ask what changed in the prototype rather than ranking people's skill. See [GUIDE-003](guides.md).

## INSIGHT-004 — Add one constraint

Experiment: build a version with no external APIs, one screen, or only six cards. Possibility: the constraint may make the first test easier to organise. State what was deliberately excluded and whether the result still helps. See [SUPPORT-003](support.md).

## INSIGHT-005 — A failed test is a useful story

Experiment: demo the original idea, the confusing moment, and the change you would try next. Possibility: the lesson may be more useful than a polished claim of success. Do not invent test participants or results. See [GUIDE-006](guides.md).

## INSIGHT-006 — Build a second use, not a bigger platform

Experiment: reuse a practice-card reveal interaction for a fictional neighbourhood phrase card rather than adding accounts or a database. Possibility: explore [COMBO-001](combinations.md) with fewer moving parts. State the new assumption and test one card; reuse is a proposal, not proof that integration already works.

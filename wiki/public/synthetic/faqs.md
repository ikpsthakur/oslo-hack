# Synthetic customer FAQs

All records below are fictional examples for demonstration. They are not confirmed Oslo Hack commitments. Status of every record: sample-only. Say “In this fictional demo…” when using these answers. See [store rules](index.md).

## FAQ-001 — Can a beginner take part?

Example answer: the demo beginner session assumes no coding experience. A newcomer can help sketch a screen, test an idea, write clear instructions, or build a paper prototype. Reference: [SESSION-001](sessions.md).

## FAQ-002 — Do I need to be a developer?

Example answer: the fictional teams welcome design, writing, research, coding, and practical problem-solving. Reference: the three illustrative [projects](projects.md). No real recruitment or team membership is implied.

## FAQ-003 — What should I bring?

Example answer: bring a laptop and charger if you want to code, or a notebook and pen for planning and design. The demo assumes participants use their own equipment; loan devices are not promised.

## FAQ-004 — Can I come alone?

Example answer: in SESSION-001, an opening round helps solo visitors find a fictional team of two to four people. This is an example format, not a reservation or matching service.

## FAQ-005 — What can we finish in one afternoon?

Example answer: aim for one useful screen, one paper prototype, or a script that solves one narrow task. A complete commercial product is outside the example workshop scope. Reference: [PROJECT-001 through PROJECT-003](projects.md).

## FAQ-006 — Must I present a finished product?

Example answer: fictional teams give a short show-and-tell of what they tried, what failed, and what they would test next. Unfinished work is welcome in the example format.

## FAQ-007 — Can you introduce me to someone?

Example answer: the dataset contains fictional project roles, not real people or member profiles. The chatbot cannot contact anyone or disclose participant information. It can suggest a role someone could try in an example team.

## FAQ-008 — Can I book a place through this chat?

Answer boundary: no. Neither the synthetic store nor the current site provides bookings, signup records, tickets, or confirmed seats. The assistant may explain a sample session, but must never confirm attendance or report a made-up reservation.

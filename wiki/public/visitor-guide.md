# Public Oslo Hack visitor guide

## What Oslo Hack is

Oslo Hack is a friendly project concept for curious people in Oslo to meet collaborators, try a small project, and share what they learn. A polished pitch or a perfect team is not needed. Beginners and different kinds of skills are welcome.

## Meet, build, share

Meet people around a question or interest. Choose an idea small enough to try in a few hours. Share what you tried, including unfinished work and lessons learned.

Possible formats include a prototype, poster, script, or service. A bike-availability checker, a neighbourhood event map, or a Norwegian-learning tool are example ideas, not confirmed active teams or existing services.

## Preparation and example schedule

Bring curiosity and an idea if you have one. A laptop or notebook may be useful. No preparation is required for the example workshop format.

The website has an illustrative plan: 10:00 meet, 10:30 form small teams, 11:00 build, and 14:30 share. Use the Show session plan button to see it. These times describe a sample agenda, not a confirmed event.

## What is not confirmed

No next event date, venue, available places, organiser contact, ticket price, language policy, accessibility arrangements, or booking link has been confirmed. The website source explicitly says "Next meetup not confirmed" and "Date, time, and venue are still to be confirmed." The expandable agenda is labelled as an example, not a confirmed event schedule.

No signup, reservation, member directory, payments, or project-submission service currently exists. The assistant cannot book places, contact people, retrieve live bike availability, or change the website. It can explain the project and suggest small project ideas.

## Fictional demonstration records

The assistant also receives a separate synthetic catalogue of sample sessions, project ideas, FAQs, and hypothetical participation arrangements. Those records are examples only, not evidence of actual events or policies. When using them, explicitly describe the answer as a fictional demo. They do not change the unconfirmed real-world facts above. See [the synthetic store](synthetic/index.md).

## Chat privacy

Chat messages are sent to Vercel AI Gateway and NVIDIA Nemotron's model provider to generate replies. The free provider may retain messages for model improvement. Do not send private or sensitive information. Oslo Hack does not add a chat database or save conversations in the browser; hosting/model services may process operational request records.
